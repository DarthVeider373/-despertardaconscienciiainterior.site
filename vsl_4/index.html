About COASTAL

A beachfront destination in Orange Beach.

COASTAL Orange Beach is a casual waterfront destination located directly on the beach at 25722 Perdido Beach Boulevard in Orange Beach, Alabama.

Guests can enjoy breakfast, lunch and dinner, handcrafted beverages, live music and Gulf views. The destination also includes a lifestyle store offering coastal apparel, accessories and beach merchandise.

  • Beachfront restaurant and open-air bar
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner
  • Fresh Gulf seafood and locally inspired dishes
  • Live music and entertainment
  • Coastal apparel, accessories and beach gear
